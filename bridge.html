Bridge

I learned to play bridge at school when I was 16. It has given me the opportunity to compete around Europe playing for the Scottish juniors. I enjoy the challenge of solving problems using logic, as well as the non technical aspects of the game such as developing partnerships, and not letting bad results affect play.

Teaching

I have taught beginners and intermediates locally since 2018. Intermediate players looking to improve are my favourite group to teach. There is so much room for growth at this stage, and players can explore advanced topics without getting into the overly complex ideas found at the highest level.

How bridge links to my academic interests

My interest in mathematics, statistics, and game theory fits naturally with bridge. The game improves logical reasoning, dealing with uncertainty and decision making, and strategic thinking, all skills that complement my academic background.
